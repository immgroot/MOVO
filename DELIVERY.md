## Scope and operational notes

Normal gameplay remains at http://localhost:3000 with server-generated random
dice and existing guest persistence. Deterministic visual scenarios ran on
http://localhost:3002 with a separate database and local-file controls. Those
controls are excluded from the product build. The QA runner is stopped after QA.

The browser provider briefly lost the four-tab session; a fresh retained tab
restored access. The report distinguishes actual browser observations, fixtures,
network tests and remaining continuous-motion uncertainty. This is a local
review build, not a production-ready certification.

The old zero-knock lap behavior is migrated once when loading legacy local
matches. The migration places those completed-lap pieces at their own exposed
gates, strips obsolete statistics and advances the revision. Guest credentials,
room IDs and deduplication receipts are retained.
